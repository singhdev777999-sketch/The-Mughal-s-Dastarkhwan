Our Awadhi Lineage

The Living Heritage of the Royal Dastarkhwan

In the gilded courts of 18th-century Awadh, the *Dastarkhwan* was sacred cloth upon which food of transcendental beauty was laid. It was here that master *khansamas* (royal culinarians) competed to produce delicacies so light and fragrant they could be enjoyed by royals of delicate constitution.

At The Mughal’s Dastarkhwan, we preserve the unhurried craft of *Dum Pukht*—trapping fragrant vapors beneath dough seals on hand-beaten copper vessels. Our signature *potli masala* blends cardamom, mace, rose petals, sandalwood essence, and 160 secret botanicals ground in small batches daily in Hazratganj.

The Sensory Theatre

Under the Open Stars: The Scent of Live Sigri & Bhatti

Step through into our courtyard veranda where the nocturnal warmth of Lucknow settles beneath lush canopy trees. Here, master *ustaads* skewering spiced kebabs work behind an open arched kitchen framed by terracotta tiles and warm incandescent lanterns.

The rhythmic clapping of *roomali rotis* tossed high in the air, the sizzling drip of marinade over live charcoal, and the aromatic clouds of clove smoke create a kinetic dining spectacle honored since 1856.
