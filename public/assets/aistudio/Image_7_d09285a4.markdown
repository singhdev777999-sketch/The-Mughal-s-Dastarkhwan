## Brand & Style

This design system translates the legendary hospitality (*tehzeeb*) and culinary heritage of Awadhi royal court dining into a contemporary, quiet-luxury digital presence. Conceived for connoisseurs, international gastronomes, and patrons of refined heritage dining, the experience balances majestic gravitas with understated modern restraint.

The style converges **Minimalism** and **Tactile Luxury**:
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep, warm midnight-espresso canvases evoke the candlelit intimacy of Nawabi evening salons.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Cadence:** Deliberate, spacious margins, staggered typography tiers, and generous negative space emulate limited-edition art monographs.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Slender hairline dividers, jali-inspired micro-motifs, and bespoke gold metadata badges pay subtle homage to Lucknow’s Bara Imambara and royal arches without succumbing to decorative clutter.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Interfaces feel physically rooted—resembling heavy textured parchment, aged brass inlays, and lacquered dark wood.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the warm embers of tandoors, brass serving cloches (*degchis*), and the ambient shadow of royal banquet rooms.

- **Foundational Neutrals:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#0D0C0B` (Midnight Charcoal / Deepest Peat)
  - Elevated Layer / Surface Container: `#171513` (Smoked Espresso)
  - Raised Surface / Card Overlay: `#221F1C` (Charred Oak)
- **Primary & Accent Gold:**
  - Primary Accent: `#C5A059` (Brushed Antique Gold) provides warm illumination for key actions, interactive hover states, and royal insignias.
  - Highlight Tint: `#D4AF37` (Imperial Gold Leaf) used sparingly for active focal points and star indicators.
  - Deep Metallic Base: `#9E7D3B` (Raw Brass) for refined hairline rules, muted metadata tags, and pressed states.
- **Typography & High-Contrast Cream:**
  - Headline Ivory: `#F9F6F0` (Champagne Cream) creates soft, dignified contrast against deep backgrounds without the eye strain of harsh pure whites.
  - Body Parchment: `#EFECE6` (Aged Silk) provides high legibility for long-form culinary storytelling and tasting menus.
  - Muted Meta: `#A6A095` (Warm Ash) grounds timestamps, provenance notes, and captions.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Vegetarian Heritage: `#4E6548` (Malabar Forest)
  - Subtle Warning / Reservation Notice: `#A85834` (Kashmiri Saffron Ember)

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances the literary dignity of historical Lucknow with international haute-cuisine precision:

- **Primary Display & Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Stately, high-contrast transitional serif. Its generous descenders and graceful italic swashes evoke court calligraphy and royal edicts. Headlines should be styled with sentence-casing or poetic titles, avoiding all-caps formatting for display serifs.
- **Secondary & Utilitarian Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, geometric, clean-grotesque sans-serif. Used with ample line-height to guarantee effortless reading across course selections, tasting notes, and reservation journeys.
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata:** All section eyebrow headers, provenance badges, and preparation descriptors use `label-uppercase` in all-caps with generous kerning (`letterSpacing: 0.18em`) and semi-bold weights for refined structure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical editorial grid that rejects dense utilitarian tables in favor of generous culinary compositions.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `5rem` outer canvas padding and `2.5rem` gutters. Course pairings and culinary narratives utilize 5/7 or 4/8 column asymmetric splits.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margin and `1rem` column gap.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:**
  - Plentiful negative space around course imagery and signature dishes (such as Galawati Kebab, Dum Biryani, and Shahi Tukda).
  - Vertical section gaps scale aggressively from `3rem` on mobile to `6rem` - `8rem` on desktop to maintain an unhurried, contemplative pace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet, atmospheric tonal stacking rather than heavy dropshadows:

- **Tonal Stratification:** 
  - Base level starts at `#0D0C0B`.
  - Nested card containers and interactive panels transition up to `#171513` and `#221F1C`.
- **Luminous Rim Borders:**
  - Panels and floating reservation modals use ultra-fine 1px borders tinted in antique gold with low opacity (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)` on static, rising to `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)` on active hover).
- **Amber Glow Shadows:**
  - When elevation is mandatory (e.g., booking trays, active tasting menu selectors), use diffused ambient backdrops: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 30px 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The architectural spirit favors crisp, tailored lines reminiscent of handcrafted bone inlays and fine marble masonry:

- **Restrained Soft Cornering (`roundedness: 1`):** Default component corners are micro-beveled (`0.25rem` / `4px`), preserving sharp structural elegance without clinical severity.
- **Signature Arch Geometries:** Feature imagery, wine cellar highlights, and private dining pavilions incorporate single-axis soft arches (`border-radius: 120px 120px 4px 4px`) reflecting Lucknow’s iconic Awadhi archways.
- **Pill Accents:** Reserved exclusively for small luxury chips, dietary classification indicators, and sommelier vintage badges.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary (The Royal Invitation):* Background `#C5A059`, typography `#0D0C0B` (bold, tracking: 0.12em, uppercase), border: none, subtle gold glow on hover.
  - *Secondary / Ghost (The Nawabi Border):* Transparent background, 1px solid `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`, text `#F9F6F0`. On hover, fills with `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#C5A059`.
  - *Text Action:* Champagne ivory with an expanding gold underline transition (`#C5A059`) offset by 6px.
- **Input Fields & Date Pickers:**
  - Seamless dark inputs (`#171513`) with subtle bottom-line or 1px hairline border in `rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.1)`. 
  - On focus, border transitions smoothly to `#C5A059` with an ivory label floating in `label-uppercase`.
- **Menu & Dish Cards:**
  - Asymmetric layout featuring the title in `Playfair Display` alongside a gold currency notation (`#C5A059`). 
  - Followed by sensory Awadhi tasting descriptions in `#A6A095` body-sm, accompanied by subtle spice notes and heritage cooking technique chips.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Background `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`, typography in `#C5A059` (label-uppercase).
- **Dividers & Embellishments:**
  - Ultra-thin 1px horizontal rules fading out at edges (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25), transparent)`), occasionally centered with a minimalist brass diamond emblem.
- **Bespoke Hospitality Modals (Private Dastarkhwan Reservations):**
  - Atmospheric dark sheet (`#171513`) framed with hairline gold borders, offering guest count selection, chef's table preference, and bespoke sensory requirements.
